# Untax

Research county property assessments and prepare draft comparisons from available records. Create a private veteran review PDF from a saved review you select, with unanswered questions shown. County records, your answers and any VA information you choose to share are identified separately. Missing building, identity, tax-year, taxable-value or filing evidence can limit the review. Drafts do not complete official forms, establish eligibility or authorize filing. Built by Moosh Works (https://mooshworks.com).

Built by [Moosh Works](https://mooshworks.com).

## Quick start

1. Follow the [Untax setup guide](https://untax.io/integrations/0.4.1) to connect a supported assistant to `https://untax.io/mcp`.
2. Ask about a property's available assessment records, a public VA office, or an official VA form. Check the response for sources and missing information.
3. Follow the sign-in link and review permissions only for a requested private review or test checkout.

Read the `untax-research` skill for the full research and private-review instructions. Use the current tool catalog and returned identifiers.
